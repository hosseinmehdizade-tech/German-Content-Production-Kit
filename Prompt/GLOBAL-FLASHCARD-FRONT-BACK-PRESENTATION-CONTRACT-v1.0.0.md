# Global Flashcard Front/Back Presentation Contract v1.0.0

**Status:** ACTIVE PROSPECTIVE GOVERNANCE, user-approved 2026-11-06; **not** an app release or evidence of universal adoption.
**Owner:** German Flashcards — Continuous Optimization Program (GFP-OPT-2026), Project Control Center.
**Scope:** New or explicitly mutable German lexical cards produced by Menschen A1/A2/B1, Aspekte B1+/B2/C1, Verben, NVV and compatible vocabulary adapters. Nonlexical Lesen/Schreiben/Grammar tasks are **NOT_APPLICABLE** unless they generate ordinary German lexical cards. Existing SOURCE/LOCKED/imported cards and user progression are not migrated.

## One authority, two separate responsibilities

1. **Content producer — required learner-visible Vorderseite:** keep the existing exact **HF2 v1.1.0** policy `Prompt/LEARNER-VISIBLE-LEXICAL-FRONT-POLICY-v1.1.0.md`, its Stage5 validator, the separate lexical-front transport gate and `de-vocabulary` transport contract. Do not replace these files or rewrite their version pins. The content must make required morphology actually available on the rendered front (generic and Study/Quick), not merely present in hidden metadata.
   - **Nomen:** reviewed article-bearing singular(s) and verified plural when applicable; support multiple genders and substantivized forms. For true `plural_only` / pluralia tantum, show the authentic plural form and **never manufacture a singular or render a redundant “Plural:” duplicate**. Legitimate non-count/missing plural receives an explicit reviewed closure (`NOT_APPLICABLE` / `CLOSED_NO_FORCE`), never a guessed form.
   - **Verb:** clean infinitive, 3sg Präsens, Präteritum and Perfekt, with correct auxiliary, reflexive and separable structure. Argument placeholders belong in Rektion/structure, not the clean lexical lemma. `typingCore` stays the answer lemma/construction, not the expanded front text.
   - **Adjektiv:** Positiv plus verified Komparativ/Superlativ when grammatically applicable and available; no fabricated comparison for non-gradable adjectives. Explicitly review absence, do not silently pass.
   - **Expressions and governed constructions:** preserve original source phrase. The explicitly source-pinned `Prompt/LEARNER-VISIBLE-LEXICAL-FRONT-POLICY-v1.2.0.md` opt-in for governed verb constructions is **only** for its registered 250-Verben workstream, not a blanket override of HF2 v1.1.0.
2. **App consumer — nonredundant Rückseite:** the user-approved **Lean Formen** behavior for Study/Quick compares each morphological fact against what is **actually visible on the same current front under its Card Design Studio settings**. Omit only exact-equivalent redundant Artikel/Plural/Infinitiv/Präsens/Präteritum/Perfekt/Komparativ/Superlativ display facts. Retain genuinely additional Genitiv, Dativ Plural, Imperativ, Konjunktiv II, further inflections, restrictions, conflicting facts for QA and any facts hidden on the front. If nothing remains, hide the **display-only** `Formen` section. Never remove source data, canonical fields, other sections or SRS answers; Typing/Audio retain original details when the front is not guaranteed visible. Preserve approved section order and separate DE/FA linguistic rendering.

**Important:** `Formen` disappearing is conditional, not a blanket rule. A nonmatching, ambiguous or unclassified fact stays visible and is flagged for source/QA review rather than silently deleted. For compact Quick, the inherited three-detail-section budget may conceal additional forms; do not claim all additions visible or change Quick density without separate approval.
