# MEM-009 clarification — One lexical card, multiple meanings, optional per-meaning examples

**Decision:** user-confirmed 2026-11-06. **Status:** ACTIVE clarification of the pre-existing `PROJECT-MEMORY.json#MEM-009-MULTI-MEANING-ONE-CARD` (2026-09-25), NOT a new competing card-identity policy or a new release gate.

**Authority and scope:** prospective new/authorized editable German lexical card content in Menschen A1/A2/B1, Aspekte B1+/B2/C1, Verben, NVV and compatible Vocabulary workstreams. Other domains only when genuinely producing ordinary German lexical cards. Historical SOURCE, LOCKED, accepted imported cards and learner SRS/FSRS remain untouched. Resolve each owner's actual CURATED_CURRENT first; no automatic old-book re-import or content rewrite.

## Binding learner-content rule

1. **One lexical target = one card with all its verified accepted meanings.** A polysemous word keeps **one stable card ID and one learner review-history identity**; its accepted, sense-aligned Persian meanings appear together on the back. Do not automatically split one card merely because a dictionary distinguishes senses. Preserve existing accepted senses, including less frequent or specialist senses where already accepted; order usual/core meanings first and contextual/technical ones later with brief labels where needed. No invented senses, forced extra rare meanings, or unapproved removals.
2. **Per-sense examples are preferred, never a universal quota or hard release gate.** For each meaningful distinct sense, aim for **one natural German example plus faithful Persian translation** when it adds useful disambiguation. A sense may have **zero** dedicated examples if none is needed or can be safely supported; zero is not by itself `HOLD`/`BLOCKED` or Stage4 failure. Several senses may share a genuinely illustrative example when justified. Existing independently applicable *card-level* example requirements, user/source exceptions, language-quality gates and actual approved examples remain respected; this clarification only removes a **mandatory example for each individual meaning**. Never generate filler, false dictionary attribution or duplicate examples solely to satisfy coverage.
3. **Bind meanings internally, without multiplying cards.** Where the actual native schema allows, assign stable *internal* sense keys (`sense_1`, `sense_2` etc. are illustrative, not forced naming) so examples, Synonyme, Antonyme, Kombinationen, Rektion/usage notes and Wortnetz relations can be attached to the correct sense when semantic ambiguity matters. These are **not new card IDs**, new review items or independent SRS histories. Do not create fake graph targets; a sense reference is not a lexical `target_id`.
4. **User-facing layout:** show all accepted meanings together in the existing back-side meanings area, readable and preferably ordered by didactic relevance. Beispiele and other existing sections **remain their existing sections**; internal sense links or concise visible sense labels disambiguate only when needed. Do not force nested tiles, new UI controls, another card template or a mandatory visible meaning number for a single-sense card. For very polysemous words, keep accepted content but avoid overwhelming the front; compact annotations or progressive disclosure would require separately approved app/UI work.
5. **Avoid semantic leakage:** synonyms/opposites must actually match a particular verified sense; related words and word families are not synonyms merely by association; a combination or example illustrating sense A must not be represented as evidence for sense B. Reuse the global related-taxonomy, lexical-relation, source-first combination, Rektion and front/back policies. Do not duplicate the same learner-visible facts across tiles without separate teaching value.
6. **Identity exception, not a polysemy loophole:** separately represented genuinely distinct lexical targets—e.g. different part of speech, homograph with independently established lexical identity, or a registered fixed expression/construction—may keep separate cards under existing source-specific identity review. Never split automatically on punctuation, translation count, semantic-sense count or identical surface; never automatically merge two independent targets merely because their front text matches.
7. **QA and completion:** for an editable successor, verify its exact current baseline, accepted meanings/IDs, meaningful delta and affected dependencies, DE/FA linguistic correctness, nonloss in projection and relevant Stage4/5/6/7 gates. Mechanical QA may report `PER_SENSE_EXAMPLE_MISSING` as an **INFO/advisory educational opportunity only**, not a failure. Broken translations, wrong sense associations, lost accepted meanings, ID loss and unresolved substantive high-risk errors retain their normal severity. No bulk retrofit, forced re-audit of unchanged/source historical cards or automatic publication/Stage promotion.

## Example (illustrative only; NOT a new required transport schema)

One card: `die Stelle`; card_id `existing-stable-card-id`; meanings `Ort/Punkt`, `Arbeitsposition`, `Textpassage`; optional DE/FA example mapped to each *where pedagogically useful*. A source-specific producer may use a `meanings[]`/sense-key sidecar; an older producer can simply keep the multilingual meanings on the single back plus its existing examples without inventing unsupported typed links. Each meaning stays on the same lexical card; no new SRS IDs.
