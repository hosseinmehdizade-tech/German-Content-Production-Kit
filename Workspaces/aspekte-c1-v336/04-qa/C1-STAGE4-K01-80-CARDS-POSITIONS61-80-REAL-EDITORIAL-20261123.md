Specific verified fixes and additions:
1. `überrennen` — original grammar described **starkes/irreguläres**. Duden calls it **unregelmäßiges Verb**, with mixed `überrennt – überrannte – hat überrannt`; fixed the misleading 'starkes' category. Split overwhelming advance and figurative inundation for existing examples ([Duden überrennen](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/ueberrennen)).
2. `die Großtante` — the old English “a sibling of one's grandparent” erroneously included a male **great-uncle**; changed all-language definition to **sister of one's grandparent**, rewrote the third sentence to explicitly illustrate the family relationship. ([Duden Großtante](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Grosztante)).
3. `namens` — the card's “called X” usage is **an adverb** in Duden. The homograph “on behalf of” is an **official/legal preposition taking Genitiv**. Previously the grammar only contrasted the sense but did not classify the first as an adverb; fixed grammatical word-class and exact source links ([Duden adverb](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/namens_genannt); [Duden preposition](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/namens_im_Auftrag)).
4. `aufzeichnen` — actual Duden has both **record/write down** and **draw/sketch a shape** senses. Previously all four examples and a single meaning only covered recording/documentation. Split meanings and rewrote example four for sketching a floor plan, with precise Persian/English. Added directly Duden-attested independent `eine Sendung aufzeichnen`, demonstrating transitive Akkusativ and not copied from card examples ([Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/aufzeichnen)).
5. `Langzeitfolge` — moved `Spätfolge` from strict synonyms into meaning-restricted related vocabulary: long-term persistence and delayed onset are not always identical ([Duden Langzeitfolge](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Langzeitfolge), [Spätfolge](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Spaetfolge)).
6. `Nichtstun` — separated imposed inactivity vs deliberately enjoyed leisure in DE/FA/EN meaning units. Corrected Persian in the forced-inactivity sentence that improperly implied unemployment. Added the independently Duden-documented `sich dem süßen Nichtstun hingeben` as combination with Dativ and full Persian/English, absent from the four author examples ([Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Nichtstun)).
7. `permanent` — separated **ständig/immer wieder** adverbial use from **dauerhaft** attribute meaning (matches examples 1–3 versus example 4). Restricted `zeitweise` as a context-specific opposite, not universal.
8. `Computerwissenschaftler` — original meaning wrongly restricted all computer scientists to **researchers**; broadened to scientific or professional computer science activity, retaining the caveat that `Informatiker` is more common but not universally the same role.
9. `Gesamtbevölkerung` — simplified an awkward plural two-country comparison sentence to a natural `Für beide Länder wurde die Gesamtbevölkerung getrennt erfasst`; official Duden supports plural `Gesamtbevölkerungen` but singular by geographic region is more common ([Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Gesamtbevoelkerung)). Reclassified word-building component `Bevölkerung` under `Wortfamilie` and distinguished `Einwohnerzahl`.
10. `Handysucht` / `Internetsucht` / `computerspielsüchtig` — bounded everyday-language usage and assessment caveat, no addiction inference from daily hours or a clinical diagnosis from flashcard wording; direct lexicographic source for `Smartphone-Sucht` and `internetsüchtig` added ([Duden Smartphone-Sucht](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Smartphone_Sucht), [Internetsucht](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Internetsucht)).
11. `Nachrichtenseite` — repaired awkward Persian calque in example four with fully aligned DE/FA/EN background reporting.
12. `Reduktion` — replaced a stiff passive emission-reduction plural sentence with an idiomatic energy-saving plural example, repaired the noun synonym's Persian gloss and grammar `auf + Akk`, `um + Akk`, `des + Genitiv`.
13. `exzessiv` — replaced unnatural excessive working hours example with excess consumption and improved the fourth example to distinguish serious training from excess.
14. `Hirnareal`, `Erholungsphase`, `Proband`, `erforschen`, `Forschungsinstitut` — specific declension and sense distinctions; weak masculine Proband; technical brain region is not strictly one ability; scientific research participant is not inherently a patient; regeneration vs general rest, related terms not strict synonyms.
