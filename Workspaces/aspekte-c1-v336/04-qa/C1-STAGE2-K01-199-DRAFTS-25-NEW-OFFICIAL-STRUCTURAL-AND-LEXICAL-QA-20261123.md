## Real language/grammar risks addressed (and Stage4 boundary)

1. **`ruhig stellen` (medical immobilization) vs `ruhigstellen` (pacification)**: both meanings and spellings explicitly differentiated, retaining source_raw `ruhigstellen`; [Duden medical](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/ruhig_stellen), [Duden placation](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/ruhigstellen_beruhigen).
2. **`Missbildung`**: Duden marks usage about physical organs as derogatory; `Fehlbildung` presented as the more neutral alternative, with a register note rather than an unmarked stigmatizing learner card. [Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Missbildung).
3. **`Weide`**: tree/willow and pasture are dictionary-distinct senses on one orthographic card. Willow primary in the Chapter1 basket-invention context; do not attribute two-sense certainty to printed glossary. [Duden willow](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Weide_Baum), [Duden pasture](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Weide_Grasland).
4. **`Sauger` vs `Schnuller`**: bottle teat/suction device vs soothing pacifier, not interchangeable across functions. [Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Sauger), [Duden](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Schnuller).
5. **`Urzeit`** has attested unusual plural `Urzeiten`; **`Sud`** has plural `Sude`. No false no-plural statements. [Urzeit](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Urzeit), [Sud](https://www.duden.de/rechtschreibung/Sud).
6. **`meistverkauft`, `Messingtopf`, `Weidenrinde`**: contextual or transparent compounds are printed source identities; independent standalone lexical documentation of each full composite is not pretended. Stage4 retains scoped idiomatic/technical notes. No automatic Stage4 approval.
